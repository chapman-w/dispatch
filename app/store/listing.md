## Description
Dispatch is the news for people who don't follow the news.

Every day you get one short edition: seven stories that matter, each explained from zero. What happened, why it matters, and the background you need, with jargon defined as you read. No feed, no endless scroll, no opinion.

Every story is built from the reporting of established newsrooms, with links to each source. Numbers in every edition are checked against the original reporting before it reaches you.

- One edition a day, at the time you choose
- Seven stories, five minutes, then three quick questions
- A globe that shows where today's news is, and an atlas of every place you've read about
- A glossary that grows with you
- Works offline once an edition has loaded
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking

## Review notes
No account is needed. Onboarding takes four taps. The edition updates once a day from a static file; if the reviewer's clock is ahead of the latest edition, the newest available edition is shown.
