Dispatch privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Dispatch is a daily world-news app. This page explains what the app does with data, which is very little.

What the app stores

Your reading progress, quiz results, the places you've read about, your settings (edition time, interests, look and feel) and the name you typed during onboarding, if any, are stored on your phone only. They are never sent anywhere.

What the app sends

Once a day, and when you open it, the app downloads the latest edition from chapman-w.github.io, a static file hosted on GitHub Pages. That request carries no account, identifier or personal data. GitHub may log the request as any web server does; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Links to news sources open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you allow it, the app shows one local notification a day at the time you chose. Notifications are scheduled on the phone; no push service is involved.

No accounts, no tracking

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset in the app erases everything it stores. Deleting the app does the same.
